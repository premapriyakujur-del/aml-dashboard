Getting started — read once, then this section stays as a reference

Step 1 — Host this file (2 minutes)

This HTML file becomes your permanent dashboard URL accessible on any device.

Netlify Drop (recommended — no account needed):

1. Open app.netlify.com/drop on your computer
2. Drag and drop the AML_OSINT_Dashboard.html file onto the page
3. You get a URL immediately (e.g. clever-name-123.netlify.app)
4. Open that URL on your phone — bookmark it. Done.

GitHub Pages (permanent + customisable):

1. github.com → Sign up (free) → New repository → name: aml-dashboard → Public
2. Upload this file, rename it to index.html
3. Settings → Pages → Branch: main → Save
4. URL: username.github.io/aml-dashboard (live in 2 minutes)

Step 2 — Google Alerts (15 minutes, one-time)

Open google.com/alerts in your browser using your personal Gmail. For each of the 22 queries in Section 6 below: click Copy → paste into the Alerts search box → click Show Options → set How often: At most once a day · Language: English · Region: India · How many: Best results → Create Alert.

All alerts arrive as one daily email digest. In Gmail: create a folder called AML Intel and set a filter to move all Google Alerts emails there automatically.

Check this folder each morning before opening your case batch. It takes 3 minutes to scan.

Step 3 — Feedly RSS (10 minutes, one-time)

Create a free account at feedly.com. Once logged in, return to this dashboard and click any Feedly + button in Section 5. It opens Feedly and adds that feed to your collection in one click. Do this for all 12 feeds.

Create a Feedly collection called AML Intel and assign all feeds to it. Feedly is available as a mobile app — same collection syncs to your phone.

Feedly shows you new articles from all 12 sources in one place, without visiting each site.

Step 4 — Twitter/X list (10 minutes, one-time)

Create a Twitter/X account if you don't have one. Go to Lists → Create List → name: AML Intel → Private.

Add all handles from Section 4. Enable mobile notifications for: @CyberDost, @RBI, @DFS_India, @PIBHomeAffairs. For state police: follow but don't enable notifications (too many tweets).

The pre-built Twitter search links in Sections 1 and 4 work without an account — they open live search results in browser.

Step 5 — Telegram channels (10 minutes, one-time)

Open Telegram on your phone. Search for and join the channels listed in Section 7. Keep all joined channels in a Telegram folder called AML. Mute all notifications — you check them on your schedule, they don't alert you.

Read-only monitoring only. Never identify yourself as a bank analyst. Never share customer data or case details in any group.

Daily 10-minute routine

Min 1-2 — Open Gmail: scan AML Intel folder (Google Alerts digest). Note any Canara mentions or active fraud type news.

Min 3-4 — Open Feedly AML collection: scan article headlines. Open and read anything with Canara, ED action, I4C advisory, or your active fraud type.

Min 5-6 — Open this dashboard: use Section 4 Twitter searches to scan live Twitter for last 12 hours. Specifically: Digital arrest India, Canara Bank fraud, ED cybercrime India.

Min 7-8 — Telegram: scan your AML folder for any overnight posts in fraud victim groups (platform names, account numbers, new methods).

Min 9-10 — Section 9 Intel Log: type or paste anything you found. Click Export → copy for Phase 3 Case Analysis Tool. Paste it at the top of your first high-priority case of the day.

This routine connects external intelligence to your case batch. An account that looks like a moderate alert becomes a confirmed STR when you know the same fraud type is spiking in that city this week.

Section 6 — Google Alerts setup · 22 queries · copy and paste into google.com/alerts

Setup instructions

Open google.com/alerts in a new tab. For each query below: click Copy, paste into the Google Alerts search box, set frequency to "At most once a day", language to English, region to India, and click Create Alert. Takes about 15 minutes for all 22. Alerts arrive as a daily email digest to your Gmail.

Section 7 — Telegram channel guide · fraud intelligence communities

Operational security rule

Never identify yourself as a Canara Bank analyst in any Telegram group. Never share customer data, account numbers, or case details in these groups. Use read-only monitoring. If a group asks for your bank employer: leave immediately without responding.
